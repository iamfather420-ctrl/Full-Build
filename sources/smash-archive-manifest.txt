## Reassembly and integrity

The intended order is part-00 followed by part-01. The reported combined archive size is 2,415,636,480 bytes. To independently verify a local reconstruction, concatenate the exact two parts in order, then compare the resulting SHA256 against the release checksum.

The working Git tree and the split ISO archive are distinct deliverables. Do not claim they are byte-for-byte equivalent without comparing their manifests and hashes.
