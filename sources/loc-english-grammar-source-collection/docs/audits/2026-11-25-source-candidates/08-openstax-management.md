# 08 — OpenStax *Principles of Management* assessment

**Assessment date:** 2026-11-25. **Decision: `METADATA_ONLY`** — retain bibliographic and provenance metadata only; do not import the textbook text or other book content into the language-source repository.

## Version and access

OpenStax lists *Principles of Management* as published on 2019-03-20 and its current web version as last updated 2026-06-11. The page does not identify a numbered edition or provide a semantic version, so record it as the current 2026-06-11 web revision of the 2019 title, not as a numbered edition. The official page links a directly accessible [PDF](https://assets.openstax.org/oscms-prodcms/media/documents/principles-management_-_WEB.pdf). On 2026-11-25, an HTTP HEAD request—not a download—reported 65,629,502 bytes (about 65.6 MB decimal / 62.6 MiB) and `Last-Modified: 2026-04-22`. The PDF URL is mutable, and its asset timestamp predates the web-version date; do not assume the PDF exactly snapshots the June web revision. OpenStax notes that PDFs are updated annually when substantial errata warrant an update. [1] [2]

The book is an introductory management textbook with chapter text and learning material, examples, activities, cases, illustrations, and references. The Preface describes those features; no separate word count was published or measured for this audit. Its approximately 65.6 MB PDF is technically feasible to store, but download feasibility does not resolve its use restrictions. [2] [6]

## Rights and component limits

The title page and its current chapter reuse notice identify the book as Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (`CC BY-NC-SA 4.0`). Under the published terms, reuse, redistribution, and adaptation are conditional: attribution is required, commercial use is not permitted, and redistributed adaptations must remain under the same license. OpenStax's help page likewise describes its textbook content as not for commercial use. [1] [3] [4] [5]

Crucially for this candidate, the book's own current reuse notice says the book may not be used to train large language models or otherwise ingested into LLMs or generative-AI offerings without OpenStax's prior written permission. This specific restriction independently rules out importing its text for an LLM-oriented language-source corpus without that permission. The notice also requires attribution on every digital page containing book content and ShareAlike licensing for digital derivatives. [3]

Do not assume every included asset has identical rights. The Preface says most art carries source/creator/license attribution, warns commercial entities to contact OpenStax about art reuse, and supplies a default attribution for some OpenStax art lacking a caption notice. The chapter reuse notice says OpenStax-copyrighted images, activities, assessments, and similar components share the book's CC BY-NC-SA terms, while third-party attributions and other permissions remain component-specific. OpenStax's name, marks, and logos are expressly outside the CC license. An image- or exercise-inclusive extraction therefore needs item-level rights checks; this report is not legal advice. [2] [3]

## Recommendation and provenance

Record metadata only, including the exact title, listed contributors, OpenStax as publisher, publication date, observed web revision date, digital PDF ISBN `978-0-9986257-7-5`, book and chapter URLs, the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 identifier, and the no-LLM-ingestion-without-prior-written-permission notice. If written permission is later obtained, preserve the permission record and its scope. For any authorized snapshot, record retrieval date, exact source URL, file checksum, source revision and the per-page attribution; do not conflate live web content with the older-timestamped PDF. OpenStax directs commercial or other permission requests to its content reuse request form. [1] [2] [3] [7]

No book files were downloaded, and no acquisition or training scripts were run. No GitHub repositories were changed; the six Library of Congress books were left untouched. Wikidata is not part of this candidate, so its CC0 terms and full-dump scale are not applicable here.
