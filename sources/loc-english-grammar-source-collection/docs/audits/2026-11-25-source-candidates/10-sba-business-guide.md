# SBA Business Guide — language-source assessment

**Assessment date:** 2026-11-25. **Recommendation: `HUMAN_LICENSE_REVIEW` before any text acquisition.** The SBA states that government information on SBA.gov is public domain and may be freely distributed and copied, requesting appropriate acknowledgement. The same policy explicitly excepts materials contributed or licensed by private parties and says the user must identify rights holders and obtain permission for protected material. The live guide does not provide item-level rights markings or a release/license manifest, so that general site policy does not establish that every element of the selected guide pages is SBA-government information. [1] [2]

## Resource and version

The candidate is the SBA’s unnumbered, living **Business Guide**, narrowed here to business-operation and customer-facing material under “Manage your business,” including its Marketing and sales topic. SBA describes the guide as helping people start and grow a small business; the management page covers 13 listed subjects, including finances, employee management, marketing and sales, cybersecurity, fraud, emergencies, and closing or selling a business. [2] [3]

No stable edition number, dated release, downloadable corpus, or fixed snapshot was identified. Treat the latest revision as **the live pages observed on 2026-11-25**, not as a versioned release. SBA’s official sitemap index listed its page sitemap with a `2026-11-24T14:06:08Z` timestamp; that is sitemap-level evidence, not a page-specific revision date. [4] For later reproducibility, use the direct live page URLs below and record each captured canonical URL, title, section, capture timestamp, and any page-level modified metadata if present. ## Rights, utility, and practical scope

The SBA policy supports reuse—including commercial reuse—of material that is verified to be government information in the public domain, with acknowledgement requested. It does not give a blanket license for private contributions, and it directs users to obtain permission for protected materials. SBA does not publish an explicit Creative Commons or other license identifier for this guide. On the evidence available, commercial use, redistribution, and modification are **conditional/unclear for this exact page-body dataset** until a human confirms which text and assets are SBA-government information and which, if any, are third-party contributions. This is a rights-screening recommendation, not legal advice. [1]

The guide contains useful small-business operations and marketing/sales vocabulary, but is a relatively small, mutable web resource rather than a released corpus. The inspected management-page extraction was about 88,000 characters and lists 13 subject areas; this is an extraction-size estimate, not a measured transfer size. A bounded text-only management subset should be technically inexpensive (likely well below a few megabytes); SBA publishes no official collection-wide size or bulk snapshot. The live management page is directly accessible, and the sitemap is a discovery aid, but neither is a version-pinned dataset. [2] [3] [4]

**Next step:** keep metadata only until a human review confirms rights for the precise body text and determines whether any material is third-party. If cleared, consider only a bounded text-only subset, exclude unreviewed images, documents, and linked-out content, and retain page-level source identifiers, canonical URLs, capture dates, and attribution. No repository changes, training, or content acquisition were performed for this assessment.
