## Rights and recommendation

The title page names **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)**. OpenStax’s title-specific online-book reuse notice conditions reuse on noncommercial purpose, attribution, and ShareAlike distribution of derivatives; it separately states prior written OpenStax permission is required for LLM/generative-AI training or ingestion. OpenStax offers a content-reuse request form. Consequently, the source is not suitable as-is for a public corpus intended for commercial use or model training. Do not infer a blanket license for third-party material: the book pages use “except where otherwise noted,” and the cited section includes a separately credited image. Any requested permission should expressly address the intended acquisition, storage, redistribution, commercial use, model training/ingestion, and excluded third-party items. [2] [3] [4]

**Repository action:** keep a metadata/provenance record only while human rights review seeks a written grant; do not add the PDF or extracted text now. This is a rights-screening recommendation, not legal advice. No acquisition script was run and no existing repository content was changed.
