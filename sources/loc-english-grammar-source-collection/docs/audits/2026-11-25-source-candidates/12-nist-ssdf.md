# NIST SP 800-218 (SSDF) — resource assessment

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-25  
**Decision:** **HUMAN_LICENSE_REVIEW — do not import the PDF or its separately listed spreadsheet until rights are cleared.**

## Candidate and version

The candidate is the English-language final PDF of *NIST Special Publication 800-218, Secure Software Development Framework (SSDF) Version 1.1: Recommendations for Mitigating the Risk of Software Vulnerabilities*. NIST’s record dates the final to February 3, 2022; the report itself is dated February 2022. Its persistent identifier is DOI `10.6028/NIST.SP.800-218`. The official PDF is 36 pages and 739,891 bytes (about 723 KiB) at the direct URL below. It is a small, feasible acquisition in technical scope, containing practice guidance, tasks, examples, and references rather than a large general-language corpus. [1] [2] [4] [7]

As of this review, Version 1.1 is the latest final/stable main SP 800-218 edition evidenced by NIST’s SSDF publication table, last updated October 6, 2026. NIST lists SP 800-218 Rev. 1 / Version 1.2, released December 17, 2025, as **Draft**, not Final; its comment period closed January 30, 2026. Do not substitute that draft for the stable version. SP 800-218A is a separate final community profile, not a revision of this candidate. [2] [3]

## Rights and component boundaries

The PDF’s own notice says it may be used voluntarily by nongovernmental organizations and is not subject to copyright in the United States, with attribution appreciated. NIST’s technical-series terms say NIST-employee works are not protected by U.S. copyright and grant worldwide, royalty-free reprint and derivative-work rights to the extent NIST may assert rights outside the United States. However, NIST also says some technical publications may have been written by third parties and may be subject to copyright. [4] [5]

That caveat matters here: the title page credits Karen Scarfone of Scarfone Cybersecurity alongside NIST authors and a former NIST employee. The cited general NIST terms do not, by themselves, demonstrate clearance of any independent third-party rights in this particular publication. The rights basis therefore is **not treated as a uniform, unconditional commercial-use or redistribution license** for a global canonical corpus. No SPDX license identifier is stated for this exact PDF. Commercial use, redistribution, and modification are rated **unclear or conditional** pending human rights review; this assessment is not legal advice. [1] [4] [5] [6]

NIST’s landing page separately offers an Excel SSDF table (about 50,039 bytes by response header), potential-update spreadsheets, and delta documents. These are separate components: this review does not extend the PDF’s notice or rights conclusions to them. Do not ingest the workbook based solely on the PDF’s rights notice. [1] [7]

## Suitability, provenance, and handling

The report is highly relevant to secure software development and software engineering, but its 36-page size makes it a narrow specialist source, not a broad language dataset. If legal review authorizes later acquisition, retain the original PDF unchanged and record the DOI, exact title, authors and affiliations, Version 1.1, final release date (2022-02-03), canonical landing page, direct file URL, access date, checksum, and page-level locators. Preserve the publication’s attribution/copyright notice and distinguish the PDF from every supplemental file. Do not treat referenced standards, examples, or linked resources as separately cleared.
