## Rights and suitability

The book’s own reuse notice identifies **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)**. Its terms allow sharing and adaptation only subject to attribution, noncommercial use, and share-alike requirements. The same page expressly says the book may **not** be used to train, or otherwise ingested into, large language models or generative-AI offerings without OpenStax’s **prior written permission**. OpenStax’s April 22, 2026 licensing update says newly released and updated titles transitioned from a mix of licenses to CC BY-NC-SA, consistent with this book’s July 2026 web update. The official reuse-request route is available, but no permission for this proposed use was verified. [2] [3] [4]

Do not treat every embedded component as covered by the book-level license. The book says OpenStax-copyrighted images, activities, and assessments use CC BY-NC-SA, while its own pages also credit third-party works under their own terms (for example, a figure on the introduction page is credited CC BY 2.0). For redistribution of the whole extracted corpus, item-level rights review is therefore needed. Attribution, source references, and page/section mappings must be retained; adaptations of covered OpenStax content must meet share-alike requirements. OpenStax trademarks and logos are separately excluded from the CC license. [2] [5]

**Classification:** commercial use is **not allowed absent written permission**; redistribution and modifications are **conditional** on noncommercial use, attribution, share-alike where applicable, and component-specific permissions. The publisher’s explicit AI-ingestion restriction is independently decisive for a language-source corpus. Recommend `HUMAN_LICENSE_REVIEW`; do not acquire or redistribute the text before written clearance for the actual corpus, access model, and intended commercial/noncommercial use. This is a rights-risk assessment, not legal advice. No acquisition or training was performed.
